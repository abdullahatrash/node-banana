# Data Residency Is Policy-Backed, Not a Marketing Inference

Tasmeemai builds region-aware storage, processing, provider routing, backups, logging, export, and deletion boundaries, but makes no MENA-local or other residency claim until the exact infrastructure, contracts, subprocessors, and lifecycle behavior are verified. A Workspace may pin a supported Data Region Policy; any provider or model route that cannot satisfy it is disclosed and excluded rather than silently transferring data. Interface Language, customer country, or Arabic Content Language never implies a residency region.

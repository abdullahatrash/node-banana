# Support Both BYOK and Managed Provider Execution

Tasmeemai will support both **BYOK Provider Execution**, where the Workspace operator supplies the credential and pays the provider directly, and **Managed Provider Execution**, where Tasmeemai funds provider effects and charges the customer under its own commercial contract. We chose a dual model over either preserving BYOK alone—which cannot reach the adopted credit, purchase, and insufficient-balance experience—or replacing BYOK with bundled inference—which would discard an existing cost-control advantage. Managed execution uses non-cash **Generation Credits** in separate expiring plan-allowance and non-expiring purchased buckets, consuming expiring allowance first; every reservation, debit, release, refund, and adjustment is auditable against managed provider usage, while currency-denominated purchases, taxes, invoices, and payment liabilities remain a separate billing boundary rather than masquerading as Usage Ledger estimates or External Provider Spend.

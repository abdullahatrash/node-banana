# Content Piece Is the Editable Creative Aggregate

Tasmeemai represents each editable format-driven creative work as a stable Workspace-owned **Content Piece** with immutable **Content Piece Revisions** that reference authored configuration, source material, Creator Personas, Workflow Runs, and produced Artifacts. We chose this over mutating immutable Artifacts, treating executions as documents, or making Publishing Plans own creative editing; the Library's Content area therefore reads Content Pieces, edits append revisions, and publishing binds to the exact revision being distributed.

# Content Piece Is the Editable Creative Aggregate

Tasmeemai represents each editable format-driven creative work as a stable Workspace-owned **Content Piece** with immutable **Content Piece Revisions** that reference authored configuration, source material, Creator Personas, Workflow Runs, and produced Artifacts. We chose this over mutating immutable Artifacts, treating executions as documents, or making Publishing Plans own creative editing; the Library's Content area therefore reads Content Pieces, every edit or regeneration appends history and new outputs, undo selects or derives from an earlier revision, and publishing binds to the exact revision being distributed. Content Piece lifecycle is limited to active, archived, and deleted, while Run and publishing state stay authoritative elsewhere; deletion is recoverable when unreferenced and blocked when retained Plans, Approvals, Deliveries, Automations, Persona training, or audit policy still depend on the resource.

# Workspace Owns Commercial State

Workspace Subscription, Entitlements, recurring and purchased Generation Credits, storage allowance, API access, Channel and Automation limits, invoices, and commercial history belong to the **Workspace**, not an individual user identity. Human roles control purchase, plan administration, and invoice access while members and authorized Agent Principals consume Workspace capacity under separate authorization, Budget, Quota, and spend-grant checks; this prevents commercial state from moving ambiguously when a person joins, leaves, or switches Workspaces. Confirmed upgrades and purchases apply immediately, downgrade or cancellation applies at period end, recurring allowance expires at its disclosed boundary, purchased credits survive cancellation subject only to disclosed legal expiry, and payment failure enters a grace period that preserves read access while blocking new paid effects; already scheduled public work follows explicit Workspace policy rather than silent cancellation. Referral Rewards belong to a verified Referral Recipient, not automatically to a Workspace, and cash payout evidence remains separate from Workspace billing.
